About Homeresi

Homeresi was born out of a simple need: transparency in the New York City rental market.

We believe that before signing a lease, every tenant has the right to know the real story behind a building. From hidden mold issues to landlord responsiveness, Homeresi provides a platform for honest, verified tenant reviews.

Our Mission: To empower renters with data and save them from "Welcome to Hell" rental situations. We aim to become the most trusted community-driven rental guide in NYC.

The Story: Homeresi is a self-funded project built by a developer who transitioned from a technical industrial background to software engineering with one goal: making the world a bit more transparent for everyone.
