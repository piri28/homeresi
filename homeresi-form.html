Legal

Privacy Policy

Homeresi is committed to protecting the privacy of every landlord and prospective tenant who uses our platform.

All information submitted through our forms — including professional, financial, and residential background details — is securely stored and processed using Firebase Firestore, a Google Cloud infrastructure protected by industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.

We use the data you provide strictly to generate the Homeresi Trust Score Report requested by the landlord. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers under any circumstances.

Access to submitted data is limited to the purposes of report generation and customer support. Tenants are never asked to provide protected information such as race, religion, national origin, familial status, or disability status, in line with our Fair Housing Act compliant questionnaire design.

If you wish to request a copy of your data, ask for its deletion, or have any privacy-related questions, please contact us at homeresicom@gmail.com.
